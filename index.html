What security services does Horizon Response offer?+

Our service areas cover static security guards, construction site security, warehouse and industrial security, event security and crowd control, residential complex and retirement village security, and after-hours security. The right scope depends on your location, environment and requirements.

How do I choose the right security service?+

Start with what you need to protect and when your site is most vulnerable. Consider access points, operating hours, visitor numbers and previous concerns. Use our brief builder to organise these details before discussing a suitable security plan.

Can security be arranged outside business hours?+

After-hours security is one of our service areas. Share your location, dates and preferred hours so the requirements and availability can be discussed. Coverage is subject to an agreed service arrangement.

What information should I include in a security brief?+

Include your site or event location, the type of property, required dates and hours, access arrangements, expected visitor numbers and any particular risks. For a construction site, mention equipment and perimeter access; for an event, include its size and entry arrangements.
