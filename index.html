Not sure where to start?
Here are a few useful things to know.

What security services does Horizon Response offer?+

Our six service areas are static security guards, construction site security, warehouse and industrial security, event security and crowd control, residential complex and retirement village security, and after-hours security services. The right scope depends on your environment and requirements.

Can you help with after-hours security?+

After-hours security is one of our service areas. Include your location, required dates and preferred hours in your brief so availability and a suitable arrangement can be discussed. Coverage is subject to an agreed service arrangement.

Is security suitable for retirement villages?+

Retirement village security calls for respectful interaction, familiarity with shared spaces and clear communication with management. Discuss entrances, visitors, common areas and the hours when a security presence would be helpful.

How do I choose the right security service?+

Start with the people and property you need to protect, your site’s operating hours, access points and main concerns. You do not need a finished security plan before starting the conversation. Choose “I’d like guidance” in our brief builder if you are unsure.

What should I include in a security brief?+

Include your location, type of site or event, preferred dates and hours, access arrangements and particular concerns. Our brief builder helps organise these details into a document you can download and share. It does not submit an online enquiry.
