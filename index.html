A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Before we begin.

Useful answers to a few
common security questions.

What security services do you offer?+

Our ten service areas include static security guards, construction site security, warehouse and industrial security, event security and crowd control, residential and retirement village security, after-hours security, mobile patrols, alarm services, campus security and healthcare security. The right scope depends on your environment and requirements.

How do I choose the right security service?+

Start with your environment, its access points, operating hours and your main concerns. You do not need a complete security plan to begin. Select “I’d like guidance” in our brief builder to organise your requirements.

Can security be arranged outside business hours?+

After-hours security is one of our service areas. Include your location, dates and preferred hours so availability and a suitable arrangement can be discussed. Coverage is subject to an agreed service arrangement.

Do you provide security for retirement villages?+

Residential complex and retirement village security is one of our ten service areas. Planning should consider shared spaces, visitors, access arrangements, respectful interaction and communication with management.

What happens when I complete the brief builder?+

Your requirements are organised into a text document you can download or copy. No enquiry is sent and the form does not store your details. Keep your brief ready to share when discussing your security requirements.
